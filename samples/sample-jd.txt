Senior Data Analyst — Lumen Analytics (Pune, hybrid)

Lumen Analytics helps mid-size retailers understand their customers. We're looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join our Insights team and own analyses from question to recommendation.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI or Tableau that leadership uses every week
- Write complex SQL against our Snowflake data warehouse
- Design, run and analyse A/B tests with the product team
- Build forecasting models in Python (pandas, scikit-learn)
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders
- Mentor two junior analysts

What we're looking for (required)
- 4+ years of experience in a data analyst or similar role
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science or a related field
- Expert SQL and strong Python
- Experience with a BI tool (Power BI or Tableau)
- Experience designing and analysing A/B tests

Nice to have
- Snowflake or dbt experience
- Retail or e-commerce domain knowledge
- Airflow or another orchestration tool
- Experience mentoring other analysts
